Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, direct answers for shop, clinic, restaurant and college owners.

How quickly can our website or app go live?

Because we maintain ready-made architectural prototypes for shops, clinics, restaurants, and schools, core setups can go live in 2 to 5 working days with your branding, domain, and catalog.

How does WhatsApp ordering and lead capture work?

Customers browse products or services on your website and tap one button to generate a structured, pre-filled order or inquiry directly into your studio WhatsApp number. Zero commission, zero delays.

Can we customize features, designs, and integrations?

Yes. Everything is built on clean HTML5, CSS3, vanilla JavaScript, Python, and Django—not bloated page builders. You can customize workflows, database schema, payment gateways, and POS.

What are Domain AI Agents and AI Calling Agents?

Domain AI Agents are specialized chatbots and voice agents trained for specific industry workflows like student test analytics, patient clinic intake, customer ticket routing, and automated calling.

Do you provide hosting, domain, and maintenance support?

Yes. We offer fully managed cloud hosting (on fast servers with SSL, CDN, and automated backups), monthly feature updates, bug fixes, and continuous technical support.
