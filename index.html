Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers in plain language.

How fast will my site be ready?

Because we build on ready-made prototype structures, starter sites are typically customized and live in 3 to 5 working days after receiving your details.

Do I need any technical knowledge to use this?

None. We configure everything for you. When a customer taps the order or booking button on your site, it opens a pre-filled WhatsApp message on your phone.

Can I use my own web domain and phone number?

Yes. We connect your custom domain (like yourshop.com) and link all buttons to your existing WhatsApp mobile number.

Can I test a sample before deciding?

Yes. Browse our free prototype catalog or message us on WhatsApp with your business type. We will share a live sample you can test on your phone.

Ready to bring your business online?

Message us on WhatsApp. Tell us what your shop, clinic or college needs and we will get back to you with a matching sample.
